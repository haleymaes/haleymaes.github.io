What a manager can do with this

Build the base plan as last year, month by month. Put inventory and cash ahead of November, not December. Hold a one-month plan about £108,000 either side of the December point. Use the −9.2% case as the default downside, and treat April 2011 (−24.5%) as a stress test rather than the planning floor. Keep the unidentified 13.6% of sales visible so an account plan built only on identified customers is not mistaken for the whole book.

Limits

Two years of monthly points. Model choice and reported accuracy use the same twelve forecasts. The 80% band is a normal approximation from a small error sample. Nominal sterling, no cost file, so no profit or margin. Guest revenue cannot enter the customer Pareto. December 2011 ends on 9 December in the file and is not scored.
